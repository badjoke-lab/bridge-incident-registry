# Blast wind-down BIR scope review — 2026-10-08

## Decision

No canonical BIR incident is created from Blast's October 2, 2026 L2 wind-down announcement alone.

## Why

BIR records bridge incidents. The reviewed Blast announcement describes an orderly network wind-down and a staged withdrawal process. It says normal Blast-interface withdrawals are planned through October 26 and that users can still withdraw afterward by interacting directly with Blast bridge contracts on Ethereum L1.

The reviewed material does **not** establish:
- a bridge exploit;
- bridge contract failure;
- unauthorized asset loss;
- permanent inability to withdraw;
- an incident loss amount.

Creating a bridge incident now would therefore overstate the evidence.

## Watch triggers

Re-open this review and create a canonical incident only if evidence later establishes one or more of:
- Blast bridge withdrawal failure beyond the announced maintenance/unwind window;
- bridge-contract exploit or unauthorized asset movement;
- assets becoming unrecoverable because of bridge or contract failure;
- a confirmed incident loss or affected-funds estimate.
