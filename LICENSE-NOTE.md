# Licensing and Attribution Note

## Current repository position

Unless a specific file states otherwise, the original written material and structured project data in this repository are currently **all rights reserved** while the public data-license policy is being finalized.

No open-data license is granted by this note.

## External sources

Bridge Incident Registry does not own linked articles, reports, social posts, regulatory documents, court documents, archived pages, security reports, or third-party databases.

Those materials remain the property of their respective publishers and rights holders.

External sources may be cited or linked as evidence. The project must not bulk-copy proprietary datasets. BIR records its own structured interpretation with source attribution.

## Quotation policy

Quoted excerpts must remain short and necessary for verification. Prefer paraphrase and link readers to the original source.

## Third-party marks and images

The initial public version does not use third-party project logos or promotional images unless their use is clearly permitted. Names and trademarks are used only where necessary to identify historical subjects.

## Future license

A future release may adopt an explicit data or documentation license. Any such change must be recorded in this file and in `CHANGELOG.md`.
